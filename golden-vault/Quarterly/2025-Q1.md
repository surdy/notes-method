## Streams in flight

```notesmith sql
SELECT n.title, s.value AS status, p.value AS priority, n.path
FROM v_notes n
JOIN v_field_values k ON k.vault_name = n.vault_name AND k.note_path = n.path
 AND k.key = 'kind' AND k.value = 'stream'
LEFT JOIN v_field_values s ON s.vault_name = n.vault_name AND s.note_path = n.path
 AND s.key = 'status'
LEFT JOIN v_field_values p ON p.vault_name = n.vault_name AND p.note_path = n.path
 AND p.key = 'priority'
WHERE s.value <> 'done'
ORDER BY p.value, n.title
```
