Design direction · July 2026

A quieter, more deliberate Notesmith

Reduce the theme catalog to three exceptional foundations: one dark, one light, and one split surface built for long writing sessions.

Every surface should communicate its role without visual noise. The sidebars organize and orient; the editor gives the note room to breathe; the chrome stays present but recedes from attention.

Design principle
Theme quality comes from rhythm, contrast, typography, and interaction states—not from the number of available palettes.

What deserves attention

✓Comfortable reading contrast across long sessions
✓Clear hierarchy without heavy cards or decoration
Polish every hover, selection, focus, and inactive state

Keep the visual language calm enough for daily use and distinctive enough to feel authored. See editor surface notes for typography and spacing details.
